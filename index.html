This is a documented, growing gap — not a one-off complaint

Multiple independent 2025–2026 studies describe the same pattern across Indian workplaces: companies push certification, then withhold the access needed to actually use what was learned.

Certification vs. access

13% offered training

vs. 33% already using AI daily at work — more than twice as many people using it as were ever trained on it.

Working around the block

40–65% use unapproved tools

Only 37% of organizations have any formal AI governance policy; 56% of employees say they lack clear guidance.

The toll it takes

51% say training feels excessive

33% feel embarrassed by their own AI gaps; 35% are nervous even discussing AI at work.
